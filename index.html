Find the right story before you write the story

Most AI writing tools start too late.

They help once you already know what to write about.

Feed to Publish starts earlier: at the moment when the newsroom needs to decide which signal deserves attention.

The platform evaluates incoming stories by audience potential, so editors can focus on the items most likely to drive reach, relevance, and return visits.

Human editors stay in control

Feed to Publish does not replace editorial judgement. It gives editors a better first screen.

The editor decides what to publish. Feed to Publish reduces the manual work required to get there.

Editorial checklist

  • which stories are emerging
  • why they may matter
  • what source they came from
  • what facts need to be checked
  • what context may be missing
  • how the story can be rewritten for a specific audience
